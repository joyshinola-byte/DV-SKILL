**Healthcare Data Visualization, Cost Relationship & Policy Insights**

**Project Description**

This project focuses on healthcare data visualization and cost analysis using Python. It helps understand patient billing amounts, insurance providers, medical conditions, and hospital admission trends.

**Objectives**

1. Build stacked bar charts and violin plots to compare billing amounts across medical conditions and insurance providers.
2. Plot temporal line charts to identify seasonal changes in patient admissions.
3. Generate a correlation matrix to analyze the relationship between age, hospital stay duration, and medical costs.
4. Prepare an executive summary with healthcare management recommendations.

**Tools and Technologies**

* Python
* Google Colab
* Pandas
* Matplotlib
* Seaborn

**Dataset**

The healthcare dataset contains patient details, medical conditions, insurance providers, 
admission dates, discharge dates, and billing amounts.

**Data Visualization**

* **Stacked Bar Chart:** Compares billing amounts across medical conditions and insurance providers.
* **Violin Plot:** Shows the distribution of billing amounts.
* **Line Chart:** Displays monthly patient admission trends.
* **Correlation Heatmap:** Shows relationships between age, hospital stay duration, and billing amounts.

**Executive Summary**

The analysis helps identify billing patterns, patient admission trends, and relationships
between medical costs and hospital stays.

**Healthcare Management Recommendations**

* Monitor high medical costs.
* Plan hospital staff based on patient admission trends.
* Reduce unnecessary hospital stays.
* Review insurance billing patterns.
* Use data insights to improve healthcare resource planning.

**Conclusion**

This project uses data visualization and statistical analysis to understand healthcare costs and patient admission patterns.
The findings can support healthcare management and better resource planning.
