**House Price Visualization, Spatial Analytics & Real-Estate Insights**

**Objective**

To analyze house prices using scatter plots, box plots, and geographic heatmaps, 
and identify high-value locations for real-estate investment.

**Dataset**
`kc_house_data.csv`

**Libraries Used**
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn

**Tasks Performed**

1. Created a scatter plot with a regression trend line for living area vs. house price.
2. Created box plots to compare prices across property grades and waterfront statuses.
3. Plotted a geographic scatter heatmap using latitude and longitude, color-coded by house price.
4. Generated a real-estate investment summary report showing the top 5 high-value locations based on median house price.

**Result**

House price distributions and geographic patterns were visualized successfully. 
High-value locations were identified based on median house prices to support real-estate investment analysis.
